FAQ

How long does shipping take? +

Orders are printed and ship in 3–5 business days via USPS or UPS. Expedited options available at checkout. International shipping available to select countries.

What's your return policy? +

30-day happiness guarantee. If you're not completely satisfied, contact us for a free replacement or full refund. No questions asked.

Are these officially licensed products? +

No — these are 100% fan-inspired original artwork. Home Soil 2026 is not affiliated with FIFA, U.S. Soccer, or any official organizing body. All designs are original creations celebrating the sport and the tournament spirit.

What sizes do you carry? +

Tees and hoodies run XS–4XL. Hats are one-size-fits-most adjustable. Check the size guide on each product for exact measurements.

How do I track my order? +

You'll receive an email with a tracking link as soon as your order ships. Orders typically leave our facility within 1–2 business days of printing completion.

Can I customize a design? +

Group orders of 12+ may qualify for custom name/number additions. Contact us via the footer for bulk pricing and customization options.
